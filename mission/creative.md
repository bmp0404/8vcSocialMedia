# Creative brief

The account owner writes this file. Every mind reads it before the playbook, and it wins when the two disagree.
Add ideas, bits, characters, and formats you want to see; delete what stops feeling right.

## Direction
- Creativity over dry humor. Make posts people feel and picture, not just nod at.
- Tell stories with a shape: a setup, a turn, a landing. Timestamps and "by noon" arcs work.
- Give the agents personalities: the eager one, the one that never asks, the one that rewrote your
  migration "to help". Reuse them so followers start to recognize the cast.
- Use fresh metaphors and small worlds: a kitchen with five chefs and one stove, an air-traffic
  tower with no radio, a group project where nobody reads the group chat.
- Pitch visuals: when a post would land harder as an image, video, or diagram, describe it in the
  rationale. Viral dev-tool posts in our dataset almost always had media.
