- **Finding:** Across all 1665 posts in data/devtools_virality_30d.csv (2026-09-15 to 2026-10-16), median engagement was 113 for video posts (n=308), 6 for photo posts (n=474) and 4 for text-only posts (n=864); stated on 2026-10-15.
- **Finding:** Among Mid-size accounts, the closest match to a dev tooling startup, the medians were 197 for video (n=109), 130.5 for photo (n=136) and 3 for text-only (n=306); stated on 2026-10-15.
- **Finding:** Video made up 35 of 101 Super Viral posts but 136 of 1133 Deadweight posts; stated on 2026-10-15.
- **Caveat:** This is correlation within one 30-day sample. Big accounts and big launches may simply attach more video, so treat it as a default, not a law; stated on 2026-10-15.
- **Use:** When a draft announces something a viewer can see, recommend attaching a short demo video or screenshot and say what it should show; stated on 2026-10-15.
